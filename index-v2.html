Who We Are

A single-point IT partner for businesses that need reliability.

Founded in 2016 and rebranded as Rapid Corp in 2021, we are a Udaipur-based IT infrastructure and security solutions company founded by Manish Patel. Our 13-person team designs, deploys and maintains technology end-to-end — from rack-and-stack server rooms to camera networks and firewalls.

We work with hotels, hospitals, schools, manufacturing plants and corporate offices across Rajasthan. Every engagement is owned by us — from first site visit through ongoing AMC.

What We Do

Six service pillars covering complete enterprise IT.

From the cabling in your walls to the cameras at your gate to the firewalls protecting your data — we own the full stack so you have one partner, not five vendors.

IT Infrastructure

LAN, WAN and Wi-Fi design. Structured cabling. Windows and Linux server setup. Storage and data backup — deployed end-to-end.

Security Solutions

Firewall setup and network security. CCTV surveillance. Biometric and access-control systems. Endpoint security.

Managed IT & AMC

Annual Maintenance Contracts. Remote and on-site IT support. System monitoring and proactive troubleshooting.

Cloud & Data Solutions

Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 deployments. Cloud backup and disaster recovery. NAS and on-premise data centers.

Hardware & Procurement

Laptops, desktops, printers and accessories. End-to-end IT hardware supply, configuration and installation.

Advanced Solutions

Computer-vision monitoring, PPE compliance detection, people-counting analytics and custom Python automation. Tailored projects on request.
